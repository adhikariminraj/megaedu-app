## 1. MEGA.EDU in one page

MEGA.EDU is one network for schools, teachers, students, parents and education organizations.

- **Schools** run their academic year on MEGA.EDU: classes, subjects, teachers, attendance, homework, assessments, report cards and mark sheets.
- **Organizations** (training providers and other education bodies) have their own verified public profile and can offer free courses on **MEGA Academy**.
- **Anyone** with a MEGA ID can take MEGA Academy courses and earn a verifiable certificate.
- **Parents** follow their children's school progress once their child confirms the link.

**Everyone uses one account — a MEGA ID.** The same account works across every role you hold and every school you are connected to.

**The top menu** (every page): **Schools · MEGA Academy · Organizations · Opportunities · About**. When signed in you also see 🔔 notifications, **Dashboard**, **My Courses**, **Sign out**, and your photo (opens **My Profile**).

## 2. Your MEGA ID and My Profile (everyone)

**Getting an account:** choose **Get Started** and register as a School, Teacher, Student, Parent or Organization. Teachers and students can also be created directly by their School Administrator, who gives them a temporary password.

**My Profile** (click your photo, top right) shows:
- **My MEGA Identity** — your photo, full name, your **MEGA ID** with a **Copy MEGA ID** button, your role(s) and email.
- **My Institutional Relationships** — every school you are or were connected to, with its status (**ACTIVE**, **PENDING**, **ENDED**) and dates. Ended relationships stay visible as history.
- **Security & Account** — change your password (current password required, at least 8 characters). Demo accounts cannot change their password.
- **Addresses** — your Current and Permanent address.

**Profile photo:** **Upload photo** (PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 2 MB). Without a photo you get a coloured initials badge.

**Notifications:** the 🔔 shows unread notifications, for example a completed certificate, a parent link request, an approval, or school news. They appear inside MEGA.EDU only — there are no emails or text messages.

## 3. Public visitor

No account needed.

| Page | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Recently joined schools, featured MEGA Academy courses, latest opportunities, educational approaches, and a sample certificate you can verify |
| **Schools** | Find verified schools by name, location, district or educational approach |
| **A school's page** | Read its profile, programs, public notices and upcoming events. **Contact** sends the school a message (it arrives in the school's Inquiries inbox) |
| **Organizations** | Browse verified organizations. **Search** by name or description; tick **MEGA Academy providers only** to see course providers. **Clear** resets |
| **An organization's page** | See its description, logo, a **Visit website →** link (when set), "✓ Verified Organization" and "✓ MEGA Academy Provider" badges, courses, opportunities, events and resources |
| **MEGA Academy** | Browse published courses. Open a course to see its **Course Outline** (modules and lessons); click the provider name to visit the organization |
| **Opportunities** | Scholarships, competitions, events and jobs from verified schools and organizations. Filter by type. **Learn more →** opens the application link when one is provided |
| **Resources** | Lesson plans and guides shared by verified organizations |
| **Approaches** | Educational approaches (CBE, STEM, Montessori…). Each shows the verified schools, published courses and resources linked to it |
| **Calendar** | Nepal's national holidays and observances, and a searched school's public events |
| **Certificate verification** | A certificate's verification link confirms it is genuine — no login needed |

**What visitors never see:** unverified or deactivated schools and organizations (and their content), unpublished courses, and any student record.

## 4. School Administrator

Your **Dashboard** is your school's control centre. Across the top: shortcut cards (**Academic Sessions & Grades**, **Subjects & Teacher Assignments**, **Assessment Frameworks**) and links (**View public profile → · Inquiries → · Homework → · Calendar →**). Below: tabs **Profile · Programs · News · Opportunities · Staff · Students · Finance**.

### School information
- **Profile tab** — description, contact email/phone, location, grades offered (**Save Profile**); **School Logo** (**Upload logo**); **Official School Address** (Province → District → Municipality → Ward); **Educational Approach** (choose from MEGA.EDU's list).
- **Programs / News / Opportunities tabs** — post, edit and delete. News notifies your school community.
- **Inquiries →** — messages visitors sent from your public Contact page.
- Your school appears publicly only after a Platform Administrator verifies it.

### Setting up the school year
- **Initial setup** — a guided 7-step setup: school year, grades, display names, sections, teacher assignments, student placement, review.
- **Academic Sessions & Grades** — one active school year at a time. **New Session** closes the year and opens the next. Students with a recorded promotion decision move forward automatically; anyone without a decision waits in a **Pending** list (never guessed).
- **Sections** — optional (e.g. 6A, 6B). They can be deactivated but never deleted. A student's section is chosen fresh each year.
- **Subjects & Teacher Assignments** — your subject list, which subjects each grade offers this year, and which teacher teaches what (whole grade or one section). Here you also name each grade's **Grade Coordinator** and each section's **Class Teacher**.

### Staff
- **Staff tab** — approve teachers who joined, **+ Add Teacher** directly, and search staff by name. Each teacher shows their current responsibilities (e.g. "Class 9 · Mathematics (C)", "Class Teacher").
- **View Profile →** opens a teacher's profile: **Current Responsibilities**, official address (you can correct it), **Family & Emergency Contacts** (admin only), plus **Manage school's subjects & assignments →** and **View this teacher's meetings →**.

### Students
- **Students tab** — approve students, **+ Add Student** directly (optionally choosing grade and section), search students, **Assign Grade & Section →** for anyone unplaced, **Change Section →** for placed students.
- **Student Profile** (**View Profile →**) — name, MEGA ID (if the student has an account), **Student ID** and **Date of Birth** (you can edit both), **Academic Snapshot** (Attendance %, Homework Completion %, Overall Performance), assessment results, evaluations (including private ones), teaching progress, test results, attendance, homework, Parent-Teacher Meetings, address and Family & Emergency Contacts, and links to the **Report Card** and **Mark Sheet**.

### Daily academic work
- **Attendance** — any grade, section and date; corrections are recorded, never silent.
- **Homework →** — see all homework; you may edit or publish a teacher's draft, but homework is created by teachers. **Not available yet:** a school-wide homework completion summary.
- **Evaluations and Parent-Teacher Meetings** — write evaluations for a named teacher; schedule, complete, cancel or reschedule meetings.
- **Assessment Frameworks** — **Create Assessment System** (a guided 6-step wizard), with **Advanced management** for later edits.
- **Assessment Results** — enter marks, review, **Publish** per subject. Families only ever see published results. Corrections after publishing stay published and are recorded.
- **Co-Scholastic** — configure areas (Work Education, Art, Health & PE, Discipline…) and grading; enter or review grades.
- **Grades / Class Overview** — each grade's roster by section, teachers and subjects, a grade-wide Top 5 from published results, and **promotion decisions** (Promote, Repeat, Transfer, Leave) applied to selected students.

### Reports and documents
- **Report Card** — a live page of published results, attendance, shared evaluations and co-scholastic grades.
- **Mark Sheet** — the formal annual result document. From a Student Profile's Mark Sheet link you check eligibility, **Issue** it, and later **correct** it. A correction creates a new version; old versions stay viewable and clearly marked. Issuing requires published results and a recorded promotion decision.

### Calendar
**Calendar →** — Annual and Agenda views. **+ Add Event** (school events) and **+ Add School Calendar Entry** (vacations, examinations, PTM days, result days, report card distribution). Items can be edited or deactivated.

### Finance
**Finance tab** — grant or remove an Accountant for your school. **Not available yet:** payments, fees or transactions of any kind.

### What School Administrators cannot do yet
Add a second School Administrator from inside the app; deactivate the school; see a school-wide homework completion summary; download PDFs of report cards or mark sheets.

## 5. Teacher

A teacher's permissions come **only** from the responsibilities the School Administrator assigns this school year. There are two kinds, and one person can hold both.

| | **Subject Teacher** | **Class Teacher / Grade Coordinator** |
|---|---|---|
| **What it means** | You teach a subject to a grade (whole grade or one section) | You are responsible for a section (Class Teacher) or a whole grade (Grade Coordinator) |
| **You can** | Teaching plan, units/chapters and progress; unit tests; subject evaluations; **enter and publish assessment marks**; create and publish homework; record homework completion; give homework feedback | Take and correct **attendance**; write **general evaluations**; enter **co-scholastic** grades; view **homework progress** for your class |
| **You cannot** | Take attendance (unless also a Class Teacher) | Enter or publish assessment marks, or record homework completion (unless also that subject's teacher) |

### Your dashboard
- **Your Academic Assignments** — each subject you teach, with **Manage teaching →** (plan, units, tests, subject evaluations, students).
- **Your Grade Coordinator & Class Teacher Responsibilities** — **Take attendance →** and **General evaluation →**.
- Today's meetings, plus links: **Your Parent-Teacher Meetings**, **Assessment Results — Enter, correct & publish**, **Homework — Create & publish**, **Calendar — View upcoming**.
- **School Students** — add Skills & Competencies for approved students.
- **Your Courses** — your MEGA Academy courses.

### Homework (Subject Teacher)
1. **Homework →** → create: choose grade, subject, section, and **Assign to: Entire Section / Individual Student**; write title, instructions and due date. It saves as a **Draft** (invisible to families).
2. **Publish** — the students who should get it are fixed at that moment (a student who joins later is not added). After publishing, the content cannot be edited.
3. **Record Completion** — mark each student **Completed, Partial, Not completed** or **Excused**, then save once for the whole class.
4. **View / Give Feedback** — see a student's submissions (text or photo) and write feedback. The student and parent see it immediately.

### Student Profiles
Clicking a student's name (in Attendance, Evaluations or Homework Progress) opens their profile **only if** you teach them or are their Class Teacher/Grade Coordinator. Your view is read-only: MEGA ID, Student ID, Date of Birth, the Academic Snapshot, results, evaluations, attendance, homework, meetings, and the official address. You cannot edit any of these, and **Family & Emergency Contacts** are shown to School Administrators only.

### Other teachers' profiles
You can open a fellow teacher's profile at your school to see their current responsibilities. Management links and family contacts are shown to School Administrators only.

## 6. Parent

**Linking your child is a request your child must confirm.** Under **Link Your Child**, enter the email your child registered with and choose **Link Child**. Your child then sees your request on their dashboard and chooses **Confirm** or **Decline**. Until they confirm, you see nothing about them. Either of you can remove the link later.

**For each confirmed child, your dashboard shows:**
- **Identity** — name, school and grade, **MEGA ID** (if your child has an account), **Student ID** (the school's admission number, if recorded) and **Date of Birth**.
- **Academic Snapshot** — **Attendance %**, **Homework Completion %** and **Overall Performance** (GPA, or average % if the school uses no grade points). These show **N/A** — never a made-up number — when there is no data yet.
- **My Children Today** summary, **Today's Homework**, and **My Homework** history (status, your child's submissions, and teacher feedback — view only).
- **Assessment Results** (published only), **Teaching Progress**, **Test Results**, **Recent Attendance**, evaluations the teacher shared with parents.
- **Parent-Teacher Meetings** for your child.
- **View Calendar →** — school dates, holidays, homework and meetings, each labelled with your child's name.

**Parents cannot:** submit homework or change anything about a child's record, request a meeting, or open the school's staff Student Profile page.

**Report Card and Mark Sheet:** you are allowed to view your child's, but **there is no dashboard button yet**. To reach them, open **View Calendar →** and click the school's **Result Day** or **Report Card Distribution** date (when the school has added one). The Report Card page links to the Mark Sheet.

## 7. Student

**Your dashboard shows:**
- Your school status, and a **Parent Requests** panel (**Confirm** or **Decline** a parent's link request).
- **Interests** — your own interests; changeable once per school year.
- **Skills & Competencies** that your teachers added (read-only).
- **Today's Homework** and **My Homework** — for each item, submit a note and/or one photo as evidence (you can submit again). You also see your completion status and teacher feedback. Late submissions are accepted and marked late.
- **Teaching Progress, Test Results, Recent Attendance**, evaluations shared with you, and your **published** assessment results.
- **Your Courses** and certificates (see [§8](#8-mega-academy-learner)).
- **View Calendar →** — holidays, school dates and your homework due dates.

**Report Card and Mark Sheet:** same as for parents — open your Calendar's **Result Day** date to reach your Report Card, then its **Mark Sheet** link.

**Students never see:** Parent-Teacher Meetings, unpublished marks, private teacher notes, or the staff Student Profile page (even their own).

## 8. MEGA Academy learner

**Anyone signed in with a MEGA ID can learn** — students, teachers, parents, school staff, organization staff, or an independent learner.

1. **Find a course** — **MEGA Academy** in the top menu (or a provider's page, or an Approach page). Open a course to read its **Course Outline**.
2. **Enroll** — use the enroll button on the course page. Only free courses can be enrolled in today.
3. **Learn** — the course page lists every module and lesson in order; some lessons include a **Watch video →** link.
4. **Complete** — when you finish, use the complete button at the bottom of the course. Your certificate is issued immediately and you get a notification.
5. **My Courses** (top menu) — every course you are enrolled in, with **Continue →** or **Review →**, and **View certificate →** once completed.
6. **Certificates** — **View certificate** shows the formal certificate. Its verification link lets anyone confirm it is genuine. The public verification page shows your name, the course, the issuing organization and your MEGA ID.

**Not available yet:** paid courses, lesson-by-lesson progress tracking, quizzes/assessments, PDF certificate download, QR code.

## 9. Organization Administrator

**Getting started:** register as an Organization, then create your organization's profile. A Platform Administrator must **verify** your organization before it appears publicly.

**Your dashboard:**
- **Organization Logo** — **Upload logo** / **Remove**.
- **MEGA Academy Participation** — **Start participating** / **Stop participating**. This is your own choice, separate from verification. Stopping hides your courses and blocks new enrollments immediately, but never deletes courses, enrollments or certificates.
- **Organization Profile** — edit your **Description** and **Website**, then **Save Profile**. The website must be a full link starting with `http://` or `https://` (e.g. `https://www.example.org`); anything else is rejected with a message. Leave it empty to remove it. **View public profile →** opens your public page (once verified). Your organization's **name cannot be changed** — it is the name that was verified.
- **Courses** tab — **+ New Course**, and each course shows **Published/Draft** plus learner counts ("2 enrolled · 1 completed" or "No learners yet"). Opening a course lets you add, rename, reorder (↑ Up / ↓ Down) and delete modules and lessons, add optional video links (http/https only), and publish. A course needs at least one lesson to publish, and a published course can never be left with zero lessons.
- **Opportunities** tab — **+ Post Opportunity**; edit or delete. The apply link must start with `http://` or `https://`.
- **Events & Resources** tab — **+ Add Event** (edit or **Deactivate** — events are never permanently deleted) and **+ Add Resource** (edit or delete).
- **Finance** tab — grant or remove an Organization Accountant.

**When your content is public:** your profile, opportunities, events and resources appear publicly only while your organization is **verified** (and active). Your courses additionally require MEGA Academy participation and must be published.

**Not available yet:** adding a second Organization Administrator, switching between several organizations you administer, changing your organization's name, paid courses, instructor accounts, and an organization calendar page.

## 10. Accountant

An Accountant is added by a School or Organization Administrator — you cannot sign up as one. Your dashboard lists every school or organization you have finance access to. **Because payments are not built yet, there is no financial data or action to show.** Accountants cannot use any admin features.

## 11. Platform Administrator

The MEGA.EDU team's own role. The Command Center shows platform counts and the waiting lists of schools and organizations to **verify**. A Platform Administrator can also open any certificate. User management and moderation tools are **not available yet**.

---

## 12. If you have more than one role or school

- **Several roles** (e.g. Teacher and Parent): your Dashboard shows one role's view and tells you which, e.g. "Your MEGA ID is also linked as Parent. This is your Teacher view." **Not available yet:** switching views.
- **Several schools** (Teachers and School Administrators): Attendance, Evaluations, Meetings, Calendar, Homework and Grades ask which school you mean and remember your choice. My Profile lists every school at once. A student can belong to only one school at a time.
- **Several organizations**: you see a list of your organizations. **Not available yet:** switching between them.

## 13. Not available yet

- **Accounts:** forgotten-password reset, email verification.
- **Schools:** adding a second School Administrator; website themes, templates or custom domains; PDF report cards and mark sheets; public verification of mark sheets; dashboard buttons for Report Card/Mark Sheet (use the Calendar).
- **Teaching:** homework marks/grades, multiple homework attachments, parent-requested meetings, recurring events, Bikram Sambat dates, reminders.
- **MEGA Academy:** paid courses, lesson progress tracking, quizzes, instructor accounts, PDF/QR certificates, grade-completion certificates.
- **Organizations:** second administrator, multi-organization switching, name changes, organization calendar.
- **Platform:** payments and subscriptions, a marketplace, email/SMS notifications, moderation tools, analytics dashboards.

## 14. Frequently asked questions

**Can families see marks before they are published?** No. Only published subject results are ever shown to students and parents.

**Why can't I see my child after linking?** Your child must confirm your request from their own dashboard first.

**Can my child see Parent-Teacher Meetings?** No — meetings are for parents and staff only.

**Can I see upcoming homework, not just today's?** Yes — **My Homework** shows your homework history, and homework due dates appear on your **Calendar**.

**Do I need to submit homework online?** Not necessarily. Your teacher records whether homework was completed, including homework done on paper. Online submission is optional evidence.

**Who can open a student's full profile?** The School Administrator, and teachers who actually teach or are responsible for that student this year.

**Why don't I see "Visit website" on an organization?** Either no website is set, or the stored address was not a valid `http(s)` link — MEGA.EDU never shows an unsafe link.

**Can a parent, school admin or organization admin take a MEGA Academy course?** Yes — any MEGA ID can enroll. Your courses appear under **My Courses**.

**Can I buy a course?** Not yet — only free courses.

**Does an organization need to offer courses to post opportunities, events or resources?** No. MEGA Academy participation only affects courses; verification is what makes your content public.

**Why did an organization or school disappear from a list?** Public lists only show verified, active institutions; an organization's courses also require it to be participating in MEGA Academy.
